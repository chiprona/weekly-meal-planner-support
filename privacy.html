Privacy Policy for Weekly Meal Planner

Last updated: 09 July 2026

Weekly Meal Planner is designed as a local-first meal planning app. The app helps users manage recipes, weekly meal plans, grocery lists, pantry items, cuisine styles, tags, and starter content.

This privacy policy explains what data the app handles and how that data is stored.

Summary

Weekly Meal Planner does not require an account.

Weekly Meal Planner does not collect personal data for the developer.

Weekly Meal Planner does not use advertising.

Weekly Meal Planner does not use tracking.

Weekly Meal Planner does not use analytics.

Weekly Meal Planner does not send recipe, meal plan, grocery, or pantry data to an external server.

The information you enter into the app stays on your device, unless your device settings or Apple services back up that data outside the app.

Data stored in the app

Weekly Meal Planner stores the data you choose to enter, including:

  • recipes;
  • ingredients;
  • ingredient categories;
  • cuisine styles;
  • tags;
  • weekly meal plans;
  • manual meal items;
  • grocery list items;
  • checked grocery items;
  • pantry items;
  • app settings;
  • starter content restored inside the app.

This data is stored locally on your device using Apple’s local storage technologies.

Data collection by the developer

The developer does not collect, receive, sell, share, or analyse the content you enter into Weekly Meal Planner.

The developer does not have access to your recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, pantry items, or settings.

Accounts

Weekly Meal Planner does not require user registration or login.

Advertising and tracking

Weekly Meal Planner does not show ads.

Weekly Meal Planner does not track you across apps or websites.

Weekly Meal Planner does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Cloud sync and backups

Weekly Meal Planner does not currently provide its own cloud sync service.

Depending on your device settings, Apple services such as iCloud Backup, device backup, or App Store services may process some app-related data or device information. This is handled by Apple and is not controlled by Weekly Meal Planner.

Deleting your data

You can delete data directly inside the app by removing recipes, meal plans, grocery items, pantry items, or other saved content.

You can also delete the app from your device to remove the app and its local data from that device.

If your device backups are enabled, copies of app data may remain in backups according to your Apple device and iCloud settings.

Children’s privacy

Weekly Meal Planner is not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children.

Because the app does not require an account and does not send user-entered content to the developer, the developer does not knowingly collect children’s personal data.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the app changes, for example if future versions add cloud sync, accounts, analytics, or other online features.

The “Last updated” date at the top of this policy will be changed when the policy is updated.
